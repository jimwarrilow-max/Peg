# Peg — launch marketing

Branding and launch material for Peg. The live, editable version is the design canvas:
https://claude.ai/artifact/U1zvrknJrYdV83AjsHugK9 (private until shared from its Share menu).

`canvas/` is a source snapshot of that canvas. The `.dc.html` files need the canvas runtime
(`support.js`) to render, so open and export them (PNG/PDF) from the canvas itself.

## Chosen direction

**Campaign: "Never do the washing dash again."** Visual style: *Pegged!* (Lilita One + Nunito,
tomato `#E8452C`, butter `#FFE58A`, mint `#BFE3C9`, outline `#1B1B1B`, thick outlines, hard shadows).

The angle comes from what Peg actually does: the risky bring-in check looks for rain in the final
two hours of your drying window, so Peg answers "will it rain before I bring it in?" rather than
"will it rain today?".
